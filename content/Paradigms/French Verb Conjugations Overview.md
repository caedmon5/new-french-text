## 3. Compound Tenses (Quick Reference)

- **Passé Composé** = Present of avoir/être + past participle  
- **Plus-que-parfait** = Imparfait of avoir/être + past participle  
- **Futur Antérieur** = Future of avoir/être + past participle  
- **Conditionnel Passé** = Conditional of avoir/être + past participle  

Most verbs use **avoir**; certain intransitive/movement and reflexive verbs use **être**.

## 5. Study Tips

- “Stem + Endings” is the key pattern.
- **Imparfait**: the stem always comes from the “nous” form in the present.
- **Future/Conditional**: same stem, different endings.
- Refer to the sub-pages for full paradigms.
